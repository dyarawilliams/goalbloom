About GoalBloom

GoalBloom is a productivity tool designed to help you manage your tasks and workflow using a visual Kanban board. The codebase is built with modern web technologies, including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap, to provide a responsive and user-friendly experience.

Purpose

GoalBloom was created to provide a simple yet effective way to manage personal and professional tasks. By visualizing your tasks and progress, you can stay organized, focused, and motivated to achieve your goals. Whether you're working on a project, planning your day, or tracking long-term objectives, GoalBloom helps you stay on top of your tasks and reach your full potential.

Features

  • Kanban Board: Organize your tasks into columns representing different stages of progress.
  • Drag and Drop: Easily move tasks between columns using drag-and-drop functionality.
  • Local Storage: Save your tasks locally on your device, so you can access them even when offline.
  • Responsive Design: The layout adjusts to different screen sizes, ensuring a seamless experience on both desktop and mobile devices.
  • Task Management: Add, edit, and delete tasks with ease, and track your progress visually.
